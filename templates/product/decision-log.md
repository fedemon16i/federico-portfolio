## How to use this log

- **Add a row** when: a decision is made in a meeting, async, or in a ticket — and someone might reasonably ask "why?" later.
- **Don't add a row** for obvious implementation details or decisions that are trivially reversible.
- **Link from tickets** — when a ticket is related to a logged decision, drop the link to this doc in the ticket description.
- **Revisit marker** — if a decision has a planned revisit date or trigger, add it in parentheses after the rationale: "(revisit after launch)".
