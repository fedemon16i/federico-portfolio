EY Fabric · Agent Analytics · Concept exploration

Closing the loop on what agents do

Inside EY Fabric I worked on the product's agentic layer, and started scoping Agent Analytics with Pendo: grading whether an agent actually completed the outcome it was given, and making that activity legible to people without burying it in text.

What this page is. The real EY implementation is confidential and the Pendo research didn't ship — there wasn't time. What follows is a from-scratch reconstruction of the concept, built to show the thinking, not a screenshot of production work.

01 · Grading the loop

An agent finishing isn't the same as an agent succeeding

Every agent-assisted step in the catalog gets a goal, not just a status. "Completed" only means something if you know what it was supposed to complete.

02 · Legible, not verbose

Transparency isn't more text

Explaining an agent's action in a paragraph doesn't build trust — it trains people to stop reading. A scannable state plus a real reason, on demand, does more with less.

✕ Wall of text

"The system has processed your request using automated categorization based on historical patterns and semantic analysis of your submission. Multiple heuristics were considered, including but not limited to keyword matching, taxonomic distance, and prior approval rates for similar items within your business unit. As a result of this multi-factor analysis…"

✓ Scannable + real reason on demand

Categorized as Compute 94% confidence
Matched 3 similar items your team already had approved this quarter.

Why this matters for the role: most of what breaks trust in an agentic product isn't a bad model decision — it's that nobody can tell whether the loop actually closed, and the explanation is either missing or overwhelming. That's the same discipline as behavioral analytics, pointed at a different actor.
